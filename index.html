I’m Abdul Aziz, a videographer, video editor and photographer based in Kuwait. I started with an Osmo Pocket 3, CapCut and an eagerness to learn. That curiosity took me to the Sony FX3 and DaVinci Resolve, developing my approach to lighting, composition, colour and sound along the way.

Today, I work on commercials, brand videos, restaurant content, podcasts and social media, with experience working alongside marketing teams and managing social media. I still enjoy the same things that got me started: trying an idea, finding the right shot and bringing it together in the edit.
